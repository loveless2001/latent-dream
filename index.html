01 / The strongest observed start
Soft prefix

Let noise enter
through the model.

Instead of filling each layer’s KV cache independently, sample 64 random embeddings and pass them through the model. The result is an artificial past built by the model itself.

The text often begins as a multilingual jumble, then turns into an explanation, a document, or a conversation. Soft starts gave the most consistently varied, non-looping samples across the two initial surveys.

04 / What the memory experiments establish

Two ways to keep the cache bounded.

Evict: drop old unprotected slots. Merge: undo rotary positions, average adjacent entries with source counts, then rerotate and add a count bias. Merging is approximate compression.

A discrepancy before reduction in one separate-container matrix pair prompted a stronger comparison: cloned caches, shared random draws, and both policies in one runtime, with a freely generating leader and a teacher-forced follower.

All 2,664 recorded pre-reduction equality checks passed. After reduction, the policies predict differently: original paired cell means were roughly 0.04–0.56 nats KL and 6–50% token flips under the same draw.

These figures do not rank text quality or semantic memory. Successful-step logits were not retained; the audit recomputed results from recorded logs. Paired results ↗

A real BOS slot helped the fragments.

Spliced real-text KV fragments imported fiction, science, code, and a political document. Adding a genuine beginning-of-sequence slot improved stability, but failed the preregistered three-part advance rule.

05 / The answer, and its boundary

A language model can run from an artificial internal past and generate text we find dream-like. In these tests, a soft prefix was the strongest starting point. Whether anything is experienced remains unanswered.

The weights stayed frozen. There is no evidence here of learning, useful consolidation, or persistent capability improvement. Text that calls itself a dream, or explains itself, does not settle subjective experience.

Small samples, mostly one stream, one calibration fixture, heuristic loop flags, and unblinded reading limit the findings. The gist-level test, instruct comparison, and 16k-token extension remain unstarted.
