The article discusses the life and experimentation of the famous biologist and explorer, Robert Boyle. Robert Boyle is often credited as the father of chemistry, but he was also an avid experimenter who conducted numerous experiments with various animals to study their motility and nervous system. He made it a habit to look for small objects which moved about quickly and kept himself busy studying them to learn something. His routine would often lead him to find himself out in the countryside, if his favorite creatures were wandering about.
