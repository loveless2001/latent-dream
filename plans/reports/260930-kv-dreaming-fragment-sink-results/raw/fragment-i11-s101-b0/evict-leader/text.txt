Call Me by Your Name An elegant, exquisite portrayal of the chemical electrification between a 24-year-old American physical chemistry grad student and his lover, an 18-year-old Italian art history undergraduate. Each gave and received to the other what the other sought to the utmost. While the very beginnings of the relationship are entangled with noise Lilla makes while speaking English, as well as a ghostly presence that wants her to play along, the physical pleasures the two encounter are transcendent simply through the experiences of their sensations and imaginings of them. After days of playing with the inquiry of miracles, the subdued sensations of wiping the coins of offspring to silence, they move far from the walls of Lilla Leuzzi's apartment, fraternally flapperily, outside, properly into the big open of a secret (and, by this point, abundantly unencrypted) desire. Erin Borgeson, 2017

这部作品生动地描绘了爱情中的感官体验和内心感受，展现了亲密关系对人们内心的触动。王赫在作品中细腻地刻画了与爱人之间的亲密互动，他不仅感受到了身体上的舒适，已经深入到心理层面，感受到一种难以言喻的感动。这种感动超越了语言的表达，直接作用于心灵。同时，他也通过回忆等方式处理了与爱人之间的时间和空间上的分离。

这是一段非常感人的叙述，它反映了深层次的人类情感——不仅仅是性欲，更重要的是爱情本身所蕴含的意义。在现实生活中，人们往往只注意到了性行为带来的生理快感，但这两者之间的关系更为复杂。真正的爱情关系建立在相互尊重、理解和信任的基础之上。
